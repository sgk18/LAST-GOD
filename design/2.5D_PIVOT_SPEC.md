# 2.5D Pipeline Pivot Specification — The Last God

## 1. Camera Architecture
- **Camera Philosophy**: Constrained-follow, delivering a tight, cinematic orthographic aesthetic while preserving true 3D spatial depth and parallax.
- **Projection Type**: Perspective (NOT Orthographic).
- **Field of View (FOV)**: Narrow Perspective (`27°` nominal, range `25°–30°`). This creates the classic "God of War: Sons of Sparta" 2.5D compression: minimal wide-angle distortion with authentic geometric parallax.
- **Starting Transform Values**:
  - Position Offset: `(0, 1.6, -6.0)` relative to the character focus point.
  - Rotation: Pitch downward towards focus point (`~5°`), zero yaw, **no camera roll** (Dutch angle = `0°`).
  - Clear Flags: `Solid Color` set to abyss void black `#05060A`.

---

## 2. World & Actor Scale Metrics
- **Base Unit Metric**: `1 Unity unit = 1.0 meter`.
- **Character Scale Targets**:
  - **Aeron (Subject A-07)**: Target height `1.80m` (1.80 units). Athletic, agile silhouette with streamlined proportions.
  - **Cyber Guard**: Target height `1.95m – 2.00m` (1.98 units). Distinct, bulkier, armored threat silhouette with broad shoulders and reinforced greaves.

---

## 3. Atmospheric Lighting Specification
- **Key Light**:
  - Type: Directional Light (`Key_Light`).
  - Color: `#6FE3FF` (cold, clinical cyan).
  - Intensity: `1.2`.
  - Angle/Rotation: `(35°, -40°, 0°)`, raking across the characters from upper-left-front to produce sharp rim highlights matching established visual lore.
- **Fill / Ambient Light**:
  - Type: Directional / Ambient Source (`Fill_Light`).
  - Color: `#0E1420` (near-neutral dark blue-grey).
  - Intensity: `0.18`.
  - Purpose: Softens shadows to prevent pitch-black crushing while maintaining high dramatic contrast.
- **Strict Temperature Rule**: **Zero warm lights** anywhere in this scene. Warm tones are strictly reserved for enemy alert states and emergency alarms in future passes.

---

## 4. Explicit Scope Boundary (Out of Scope for this Pass)
To preserve pipeline integrity and focus on 3D asset/camera pipeline verification, the following systems are **strictly OUT OF SCOPE**:
- ❌ No combat execution or attack triggers.
- ❌ No dialogue, typewriter UI, or text banners.
- ❌ No cutscene sequence managers or timeline director triggers.
- ❌ No walk, run, dash, or jump locomotion animations.
- ❌ No guard AI states beyond static looped `Idle`.
